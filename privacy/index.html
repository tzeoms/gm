Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This policy explains what happens to your data when you use Gamermaid's tools. We try to keep things simple: most tools run entirely in your browser, and we only send data to a third party where that is genuinely required for the tool to work.

Image Studio tools

The Image Studio tools (upscaler, converter, resizer, compressor, cropper) process your images entirely client-side, in your browser. Your images are never uploaded to a Gamermaid server or any third party — all processing happens locally on your device using your browser's own capabilities.

Social Media Studio tools

The Social Media Studio tools (caption, hashtag, bio, script, and similar generators) send only the text you enter into the relevant form fields to a third-party AI API endpoint in order to generate the requested content. We do not attach any additional personal information to these requests beyond what you type into the form.

Account sign-in

If you choose to create an account or sign in, authentication is handled by Firebase Authentication (including "Continue with Google"). Firebase manages your credentials and session on our behalf — we do not store passwords ourselves. If authentication is not configured on a given deployment of this site, sign-in is disabled and no account data is collected.

Local storage

Gamermaid uses your browser's localStorage to remember preferences (such as reduced motion or theme settings), recently used tools, and favorite tools. This information stays on your device and is not transmitted to our servers. Clearing your browser data or using a different browser/device will reset it.

Cookies and tracking

Gamermaid does not use advertising trackers or third-party analytics cookies. Any cookies set are limited to what is required for Firebase Authentication to function when you are signed in.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time as the site's features change. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@gamermaid.site.
